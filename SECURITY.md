# Security & Regulatory Safeguards — QuantAI

**Author:** `yuva.devlab@gmail.com`

---

## 1. Capital Protection & Zero-Broker Invariant

1. **Air-Gapped Order Placement**:
   - The LLM and AI Copilot have **ZERO** direct access to broker execution endpoints (Zerodha Kite, Upstox, Angel One, Dhan).
   - In live trading modes (Phase 3), execution requires deterministic pre-trade risk engine validation and multi-factor human authorization.
2. **Prediction Lab Isolation**:
   - The Prediction Lab is an analytical evaluation engine, not an execution broker. Predictions are simulated in paper environments to benchmark AI reliability without risking real capital.

---

## 2. Financial Computation & Data Integrity

- **Fixed-Point / Cent Arithmetic**: All financial calculations (P&L, margin requirements, commission, MFE/MAE) must avoid careless JavaScript floating-point errors (`0.1 + 0.2 !== 0.3`).
- **No Hallucinated Data**: Market prices, order book levels, and technical indicators are computed exclusively by deterministic math services and never generated by language models.
